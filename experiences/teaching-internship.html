Overview

During my teaching internship at University Laboratory School & College, I worked with students from Grades 6–10, teaching both English and Bangla. The internship provided practical classroom experience in lesson planning, instruction, student engagement, assessment, and the preparation of learning materials.

Teaching Responsibilities

I developed and delivered lessons with attention to student participation and learning outcomes. Working across different grade levels required adapting lesson content, classroom activities, and instructional approaches to suit students’ varying learning needs.

Lesson Planning & Learning Materials

As part of the internship, I designed classroom materials and teaching aids to support instruction. This experience helped me connect educational theory with classroom practice and think more carefully about how lesson structure and learning activities can support student engagement.

Assessment Design

I developed assessment rubrics using Bloom’s Taxonomy, aligning classroom activities and assessment with different levels of learning. This strengthened my understanding of how assessment can be used to evaluate learning while also supporting the instructional process.
